# World record: the carved city of the makers

Kind: world-record
Summary: A dead stone city under ice whose walls hold the whole history of the race that built it. Its makers bred a shapeless labour species, lost the arts that controlled it and retreated underground, and the servants now hold the depths and carve crude copies of their masters' work.

Record of a fictional world read in part: the wrecked camp, the entry into the city, the history on its
walls, and the descent and flight. Confidence: `high` = stated in the text; `medium` = inferred from what
the text shows.

## World in one paragraph

Beyond a mountain wall at the coldest end of the world lies a vast dead city, sealed in ice and carved on
nearly every wall. An ancient race built it after an age of older cities and wars. They bred shapeless
servants for heavy work, put down their rebellion and bred them again. As the cold came they moved down
to a warm buried sea. Explorers read this on the walls while a few frozen makers, dug up by an advance
party and revived, cross the same ground, reach the depths and are killed by what their servants have become.

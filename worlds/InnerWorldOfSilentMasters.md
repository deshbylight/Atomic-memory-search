# World record: the inner world of the silent masters

Kind: world-record
Summary: A land on the inside of a hollow planet under a sun that never moves, so time and direction cannot be measured. A deaf reptile race rules through ape-like guards who sign for it and interpret to everyone else, while tribes hide behind trails that break off. Those in power never notice the people who carry their loads.

## World in one paragraph

A boring machine goes wrong and comes out on the inside of a hollow planet. A small sun hangs fixed at the centre, so
there is no night and no horizon, and the land curves up into the haze. Nobody can measure time or name a direction.
A winged reptile race that has no hearing holds cities cut into limestone. It signs to its guards, the guards interpret
to everyone else, and it treats people as animals that cannot think. Tribes survive apart, in tree villages, in
cliff caves and behind trails built to mislead. Two outsiders are put to carrying and shelving in the masters' archive.
They learn to read it, find that the race depends on one book kept in one cellar, carry it off, and then teach the
tribes to make weapons and form a federation.

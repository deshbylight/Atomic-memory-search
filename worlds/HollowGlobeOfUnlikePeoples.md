# World record: the hollow globe of unlike peoples

Kind: world-record
Summary: A traveller drops through a cave into a hollow earth with its own sun, and passes from a country of slow walking trees to a country of quick apes to a shore he conquers, each people judging him by opposite measures.

Record of a fictional world read in whole, from the in-fiction preface to the last page of the story; only the publisher's header, a modern biographical introduction, the footnotes and the licence text were left out. Confidence: `high` = stated in the text; `medium` = inferred from what the text shows.

## World in one paragraph

A young graduate lowered on a rope into a breathing cave falls when the rope gives way, and finds the earth hollow, with an inner sun, planets and a lit inner shell. On a small planet the citizens are walking, reasoning trees, for whom slowness is judgement; his quick wits make him fit only to run errands. His survey of the planet shows a chain of lands, each ruled by one odd measure. A law he proposes fails, and he is carried off by great birds to the inner shell, where quick apes count him dull. He rises, falls to a false accusation, is shipwrecked, and on a backward shore teaches arms, conquers his neighbours and is crowned. Power makes him cruel; his people rise, and fleeing through a cave he comes out on the surface where he started, and every year climbs back to gaze into it.

# World record: drift of peoples between catastrophes

Kind: world-record
Summary: An invented history in which three catastrophes and a steady chain of migrations move peoples across a continent. What survives each fall is decided by position, materials, and who pushed whom, and almost all memory of it is lost.

Record of a fictional world read in whole. Confidence: `high` = stated in the text; `medium` =
inferred from what the text shows.

## World in one paragraph

Old kingdoms hire foreign soldiers and crumble. A catastrophe sinks their lands and raises new
mountains, sparing the edges. The survivors fall back to stone. Over thousands of years new peoples
drift out of the north, build, conquer and mix. A great empire wrecks the frontier people who shielded
it and is overrun by the wild clans it had armed and trained. Cold drives another wave south, a last
catastrophe breaks the continent, and nothing remains but a few place names attached to legends.

## Unresolved

- The text is a survey with one extended scene, so it says little about how daily life looked.
- Why some peoples adopted the ways of those they conquered and others did not is never explained.

# World record: caverns under the drowned courts

Kind: world-record
Summary: A sealed door in a sinking ruin leads to a lit cavern of castes, a thing that feeds on the living, and its makers who will not destroy it.

Record of a fictional world read in whole, from the frame foreword to the last page of the story; only the publisher's header, a transcriber's note and licence text were left out. Confidence: `high` = stated in the text; `medium` = inferred from what the text shows.

## World in one paragraph

Walled islets on a slowly sinking ocean land hide a slab that opens only to moonlight. Behind it a basin under seven beams gives form to a radiant thing that carries off one victim a night. A sloped way and a car lead into a cavern lit by its own cliffs, where a fair ruling caste, soldiers and a despised digging caste serve the thing through a priestess and a council. Its makers, three ancient beings under a sentence, sit beyond a red sea and a bridge over a bottomless pit, tended by a people of frog folk. The old masters of the place built the thing, could not kill it, sealed the way and left. The story ends with a battle on the bridge, a rising of the low caste, the destruction of the thing and a fall of ground that closes the road for good.

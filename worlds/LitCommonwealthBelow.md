# World record: the lit commonwealth below

Kind: world-record
Summary: Below a mine lies a lit country without night, whose people hold a force that makes any one of them lethal to any other, and so have no war, no law and nothing left to write.

Record of a fictional world read in whole, from the title page to the last page of the story; only the publisher's header and licence text were left out. Confidence: `high` = stated in the text; `medium` = inferred from what the text shows.

## World in one paragraph

A new mine shaft breaks into a charred chasm, and one survivor comes down into a country with a cavern roof for a sky and lamps for a sun. Its people went below ahead of a slow flood, and centuries later mastered one force, carried in a staff, that heals, cuts rock and can burn a city. Since anyone can now kill anyone, war, law, rank and ambition have gone. Communities split when full, children do the dangerous work and guard the borders, and old art is better than anything new. The stranger stays as a guest who is never left alone, until the love of a chief's daughter condemns him. She cuts a new cleft by night and carries him up. He writes because their own legend says they will return and replace everyone else.

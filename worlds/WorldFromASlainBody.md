# World record: the world made from a slain body

Kind: world-record
Summary: A cosmos cut from the corpse of the first giant, held together by a great tree that its keepers must tend every day, ruled by a small society of gods whose peace rests on oaths, pledges and fetters. Every safeguard has one gap, and the gaps are found.

Record of a fictional world read in whole, including its scholarly notes; only the title matter, the word list and the
name index were left out. Confidence: `high` = stated in the text; `medium` = inferred from what the text shows.

## World in one paragraph

Before anything there was an empty gap between a place of fire and a place of frost. A giant formed there, was
killed, and his flesh, blood, bones and skull became land, sea, rock and sky. Small creatures that bred in the
corpse were given minds and became the smiths under the hills. A vast tree joins the levels of this world, and
three keepers water it each day so that it will not rot. A small ruling society holds its place by a chain of
bargains: an unpaid builder, a monster bound by trickery, a favourite made safe by an oath. Each bargain leaves a
gap, each gap brings a death or a feud, and the whole order is known in advance to end in a last battle.

# World record: the people under the mine

Kind: world-record
Summary: A mountain shared by miners and a people driven underground long ago. The two work different hours, hear each other through the rock, and the hidden people carry out a prepared plan, then a second one, which their own tunnel turns against them.

Record of a fictional world read in part: the sections on the underground people, the mines, their
councils and the flood. Confidence: `high` = stated in the text; `medium` = inferred from what the
text shows.

## World in one paragraph

A mountain country has mines dug for rich ore, and the digging keeps breaking into older natural
caverns. A people who once lived above and left, for reasons nobody agrees on, live in those caverns.
They have changed in body, grown cleverer, and kept one grudge. They work when the miners are gone.
They have a ruler, a council and two plans against the people above. A young miner listens through
the rock and learns enough for the mine to be walled in time.

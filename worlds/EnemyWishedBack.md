# World record: the enemy wished back

Kind: world-record
Summary: Lords of an island realm fight a sorcerer-king's empire across the world: one of them is taken by a sending, the others climb an unclimbed peak and ride a hatched steed to free him, their home is occupied and won back, and when the empire falls the victors grieve until the gods restore their enemy.

## World in one paragraph

A sorcerer-king rules from a black fortress on a fen, and each king of his line seems the same soul in a new body. After his champion dies in a wrestling bout, he raises a power in a sealed tower room and carries off the best warrior of the island lords to a frozen peak. The lords cross the sea, are wrecked, and cross a haunted desert where three ensorcelled hosts have chased each other round one old fort for nine years. They climb two great peaks under a curse of order, kill a solitary man-eater on a cliff, and reach a hidden queen inside a mountain who tells them a steed must be hatched to reach the captive. Meanwhile the empire overruns their home, sacks a castle, and is driven out again by a horse force that crosses a storm ridge by night. The captive is freed, the empire's great fleet dies in a strait, its army breaks before the fortress, and a second conjuring by the king brings his tower down. The victors, with nothing left to fight, wish their enemy back, and the story ends where it began.

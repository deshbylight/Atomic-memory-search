## K10-21

**Frame proportions and internal spacing are separate variables.** A composition exercise can hold the rectangle fixed while varying internal spacing, then vary the rectangle around a related theme.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: framing; Framing, cropping and off-screen implication; Thumbnail exploration and selection.
- Apply: Compare both studies before choosing a concept image format.
- Limit: No aspect ratio guarantees an effective composition.
- Check: At matched display size, check whether the focal relation survives each boundary change.
- Requires [K10-01](VisualLanguage.md#k10-01): Compare the intended hierarchy in every variant.
- Supports [K10-04](VisualLanguage.md#k10-04): Controlled variations can preserve a common visual theme.

## K10-22

**A middle value adds another mass assignment.** Three-value studies distribute light, middle and dark masses, adding a distinct tone relationship to a binary study.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: value grouping; Value grouping and thumbnail reads; Silhouette, grayscale and palette checks.
- Apply: Keep outlines fixed and compare alternative middle-tone placements.
- Limit: Three values are an exercise constraint, not a required final rendering style.
- Check: Reduce each study to a thumbnail; reject a middle tone that merges a required separation.
- Requires [K10-12](VisualLanguage.md#k10-12): A binary mass study supplies a comparison baseline.
- Constrains [K10-17](VisualDepth.md#k10-17): Adjacent equal values can remove a needed boundary.

## K10-23

**Line treatment has a medium and scale.** Outline can vary in weight and emphasis; a treatment suited to one reproduction method need not suit another.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: Viewing conditions; Line weight and edge treatment; Hierarchy at target camera distance.
- Apply: Specify line weight at the intended output size instead of copying a study at arbitrary magnification.
- Limit: A characteristic line treatment does not guarantee recognition.
- Check: Inspect the reduced output for lost marks and merged neighboring contours.
- Supports [K10-07](VisualLanguage.md#k10-07): Contour readability can be checked without internal detail.
- Requires [K10-03](VisualLanguage.md#k10-03): Judging marks needs a stated viewing condition.

## K10-24

**Furniture and wear delimit useful wall ornament.** Furniture can conceal lower wall decoration, while contact and soiling motivate protective treatment near the floor.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: Wear as a readable pattern; Style compatibility with world rules.
- Apply: Place essential motifs above expected obstructions and distinguish protected surfaces from exposed decoration.
- Limit: The suitable height and material depend on actual use.
- Check: Overlay furniture and contact zones; flag hidden essential motifs and unexplained pristine edges.
- Requires [K10-08](VisualLanguage.md#k10-08): The visible receiving surface constrains the motif.
- Supports [K12-02](EnvironmentArt.md#k12-02): Contact zones can explain localized surface change.

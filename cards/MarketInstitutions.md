## K04-15

**A higher unit price can lower total revenue.** Revenue is price times quantity sold; a price rise can reduce revenue when sales respond strongly enough. Elasticity measures proportional responsiveness.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: pricing; sensitivity analysis.
- Apply: Compare purchases and total spending at several nearby prices.
- Limit: Revenue excludes costs, and a response estimate need not hold at another price.
- Check: Recompute both revenue and profit after changing the price; flag unchanged assumed sales.
- Requires [K04-06](EconomyAndProgression.md#k04-06): Alternative prices and costs can change the buyer response.
- Supports [K04-14](EconomyAndProgression.md#k04-14): Additional units need not retain the same value to a buyer.

## K04-16

**Price information changes where traders deliver.** Local price differences can persist when traders lack timely information; better information can redirect deliveries and reduce unsold perishable goods.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: pricing; NPC and player markets; exploits and arbitrage.
- Apply: Compare quoted prices with travel costs, spoilage and the price after delivery.
- Limit: A quoted price gap alone does not establish profitable repeatable arbitrage.
- Check: Repeat the trade with a larger cargo and updated demand; check whether the margin survives.
- Requires [K04-05](EconomyAndProgression.md#k04-05): Travel consumes resources and displaces another use of time.
- Requires [K04-15](MarketInstitutions.md#k04-15): More supply need not retain the same selling price and revenue.

## K04-17

**Rivalry and exclusion are separate resource properties.** One user can leave a benefit available to others while access can still be restricted; non-rival use and excludability describe different properties.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: scarcity; NPC and player markets.
- Apply: Separate a shared recipe from scarce ingredients and permission to learn it.
- Limit: Copying information can be cheap while producing, teaching or delivering it remains costly.
- Check: Add a user, then deny access: identify which action changes availability and which changes permission.
- Supports [K05-05](CooperativePlay.md#k05-05): Shared benefits can separate contribution from access.
- Supports [K08-13](Societies.md#k08-13): An allocation institution can control access independently of production.

## K04-18

**Private production cost can omit harm to others.** Production can impose costs on people outside the producing decision; marginal social cost includes both private cost and external harm.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: sources and sinks; crafting chains.
- Apply: Trace a workshop byproduct to its affected livelihood as well as its profitable output.
- Limit: A harmful effect needs a causal path; higher profit alone does not prove an externality.
- Check: Compare the producer account with downstream losses and who can change the decision.
- Requires [K07-10](PhysicalWorld.md#k07-10): For nutrient runoff, the ecological harm needs its own mechanism.
- Constrains [K05-09](CooperativePlay.md#k05-09): Judging an allocation needs affected participants as well as the producer.

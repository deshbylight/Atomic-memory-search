## K03-16

**Concealment and protection block different paths.** An obstacle can block vision, projectiles and movement in different combinations; concealment alone need not stop an attack.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: attack and defense; visibility and exposure; hiding and escape.
- Apply: Describe each barrier with three separate blocking properties.
- Limit: Material appearance does not establish collision or penetration rules.
- Check: Trace an observer, a shot and a moving body through the same barrier.
- Supports [K03-07](ActionsAndCombat.md#k03-07): Detection evidence must match what the obstacle actually hides.
- Supports [K03-03](ActionsAndCombat.md#k03-03): Different blocking properties create different positional choices.

## K03-17

**Cover height depends on posture and attack angle.** Low cover can support observation or firing over it while requiring a crouched posture for protection; elevated attacks change its exposed area.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: attack and defense; range and positioning.
- Apply: Draw standing, crouched and elevated sightlines before sizing a barrier.
- Limit: A height category alone does not guarantee safety.
- Check: From each attack angle, mark exposed body regions during observation and firing.
- Requires [K03-16](EncounterTactics.md#k03-16): The barrier must block the relevant attack path.
- Requires [K06-03](SpatialDesign.md#k06-03): Sightlines and available reach define the usable position.

## K03-18

**An arena preview can support commitment.** A chance to survey an arena before engagement can help players identify fighting positions and retreat options instead of remaining at the entrance.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: timing and commitment; range and positioning.
- Apply: Expose useful positions before demanding a commitment to the fight.
- Limit: A visible preview may go unnoticed or become outdated after reinforcements.
- Check: Observe whether a newcomer identifies a usable position and moves beyond the doorway.
- Requires [K03-16](EncounterTactics.md#k03-16): A preview should distinguish protection from mere concealment.
- Requires [K02-12](PlayerExperience.md#k02-12): Visible layout information still needs attention.

## K03-19

**Enemy combinations add relationships to manage.** Combining enemy types creates interactions between their demands; a large palette can obscure which threat or response matters.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: attack and defense; timing and commitment; enemy intent.
- Apply: Compare each enemy alone with the pair before adding another type.
- Limit: No fixed number of enemy types guarantees readability or difficulty.
- Check: Check whether a response to one threat exposes the player to the other and whether that conflict is readable.
- Requires [K03-05](ActionsAndCombat.md#k03-05): Each threat needs a usable response before combining demands.
- Constrains [K02-12](PlayerExperience.md#k02-12): Concurrent threats compete for the attention needed to read them.

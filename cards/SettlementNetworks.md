## K11-19

**Freight adjacency reduces intermediate handling.** Connecting workshops and warehouses directly to freight transport can reduce intermediate cartage, handling and heavy street traffic.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: Food hinterlands, trade routes and transport; Service, production and residential adjacency.
- Apply: Trace a cargo from arrival through storage and production to dispatch.
- Limit: A nearby route is useful only with workable loading access and capacity.
- Check: Count transfers and street crossings for a representative bulky delivery.
- Requires [K04-05](EconomyAndProgression.md#k04-05): Handling and transport consume time available for other work.
- Supports [K11-02](BuiltWorld.md#k11-02): Freight connections explain a distinct service movement network.

## K11-20

**Utility access changes the disruption of repairs.** Accessible service passages can allow inspection and repair of utility lines without repeatedly opening the street above.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: infrastructure.
- Apply: Provide a route for the repairer, tools and replacement sections.
- Limit: A service tunnel has its own construction and maintenance costs.
- Check: Locate a leak and trace repair access while the street remains occupied.
- Supports [K11-14](BuiltWorld.md#k11-14): Repair access must reach the actual distribution branch.
- Supports [K11-16](InteriorSequence.md#k11-16): Maintenance paths also need workable transitions at entrances.

## K11-21

**An exchange center can follow arriving trade.** A maritime settlement can place its principal exchange space near the harbor, while an inland center may serve converging internal routes.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: Food hinterlands, trade routes and transport; Institutions, centers and landmarks.
- Apply: Locate the market by tracing arrivals and onward destinations.
- Limit: This is a possible siting logic, not a universal city layout.
- Check: Move the arrival point and compare the journeys to the exchange space.
- Requires [K11-19](SettlementNetworks.md#k11-19): Goods still need a workable path between landing and storage.
- Supports [K11-02](BuiltWorld.md#k11-02): Exchange siting changes ordinary access to a settlement service.

## K11-22

**Public space dimensions depend on its gatherings.** A civic square serves expected attendance and activities; spectacle, circulation and surrounding business can require different clearances and edges.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: Settlement purpose and supporting population; Districts, blocks, streets and public space.
- Apply: Compare an ordinary trading day with a crowded public event.
- Limit: No historical rectangle or population ratio establishes universal capacity.
- Check: Sketch the gathering, sightlines and circulation at both attendance levels.
- Requires [K11-01](BuiltWorld.md#k11-01): The activities determine which spatial clearances matter.
- Constrains [K11-21](SettlementNetworks.md#k11-21): A convenient trade location still needs room for its gatherings.

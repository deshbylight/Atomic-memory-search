## K08-24

**A religious office carries duties beyond the ceremony.** An institutional religious specialist can hold a continuing rank and recognized duties even when no ritual is being performed.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: hierarchy; Religious specialists and institutions.
- Apply: Separate ceremonial work, instruction and the office's everyday jurisdiction.
- Limit: Titles and divisions of work vary; one specialist may combine several roles.
- Check: Describe an ordinary day without a ceremony and identify which duties and authority remain.
- Requires [K08-07](Societies.md#k08-07): A continuing office still needs limits on what its holder may decide.
- Supports [K08-06](Societies.md#k08-06): Specialist duties can explain who maintains everyday religious practices.

## K08-25

**A call for renewal can claim authority outside an office.** A prophetic leader can call for religious change through a claimed revelation or exceptional personal authority rather than an established institutional rank.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: Religious specialists and institutions; Orthodoxy, heresy and internal diversity.
- Apply: Distinguish an office holder's mandate from a reformer's claim and the followers who recognize it.
- Limit: A claim to revelation does not establish its truth, public acceptance or inevitable conflict.
- Check: Remove the formal title and identify why any followers would still accept the message.
- Requires [K08-08](Societies.md#k08-08): Personal allegiance and institutional succession can sustain different claims.
- Constrains [K08-24](ReligiousAuthority.md#k08-24): A call for renewal and the maintenance of an office are different grounds for a role.

## K08-26

**Religious affiliation does not specify commitment.** People sharing a religious affiliation can differ in doctrinal knowledge, participation and conviction; an identity label does not measure all three.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: Orthodoxy, heresy and internal diversity; variation within a society; Avoiding universalized stereotypes.
- Apply: Give two affiliated inhabitants different patterns of attendance, understanding and commitment.
- Limit: Variation does not imply insincerity or that no shared teaching exists.
- Check: Remove the affiliation label and compare the evidence for each person's practice and beliefs.
- Requires [K08-16](CulturalChange.md#k08-16): An expressed identity must be compared with conduct rather than treated as its cause.
- Supports [K09-13](Narrative.md#k09-13): Differences between a label and consequential actions can individualize a character.

## K08-27

**Religious coexistence need not erase distinctions.** People can accept the presence or value of another faith while retaining distinct practices; coexistence and adopting a shared hybrid practice are separate responses.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: Orthodoxy, heresy and internal diversity; Variation within cultures.
- Apply: Distinguish shared civic access from shared worship when describing neighboring communities.
- Limit: Official acceptance does not establish equal treatment in every encounter or agreement about doctrine.
- Check: Name a difference that remains accepted and a concrete situation in which that acceptance is tested.
- Requires [K08-26](ReligiousAuthority.md#k08-26): Responses to another faith can vary among members of the same affiliation.
- Constrains [K08-19](CulturalChange.md#k08-19): Contact can produce hybrid practices, but contact alone does not require them.

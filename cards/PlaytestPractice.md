## K16-16

**Assistance changes what a successful trial demonstrates.** Unprompted play and play explained by a facilitator provide different evidence about independent comprehension.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: Task scenarios, instructions and facilitator effects; observations; Think-aloud, behavioral observation and qualitative coding.
- Apply: Record interventions and preserve the behavior observed before each hint.
- Limit: Intervening in a broken or stuck session can be appropriate; assisted completion remains assisted.
- Check: Compare the claimed independent success with the intervention log and the last unaided action.
- Requires [K16-15](ResearchAndProduction.md#k16-15): Different assistance can explain condition differences.
- Constrains [K06-15](SpatialLearning.md#k06-15): Hinted success cannot establish transfer with fewer prompts.

## K16-17

**Noticing, interpreting and liking are different questions.** A debrief can ask whether a cue was noticed, what it meant and how the experience felt; these answers address different claims.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: Interviews, surveys and field observation; observations; Observable behavior versus reported preference.
- Apply: Ask about the observed choice before revealing its intended explanation.
- Limit: Retrospective answers can be incomplete and do not replace observed behavior.
- Check: Match each claim to an action or answer; flag preference used as evidence of comprehension.
- Requires [K16-05](ResearchAndProduction.md#k16-05): The question must measure the intended property.
- Requires [K02-12](PlayerExperience.md#k02-12): Being visibly present does not establish noticing.

# Concept knowledge cards

Short cards of game, world, spatial and visual design knowledge, grouped into 16 groups. Each card states one
principle and separates it from its untested application: Kind (general guidance or design inference), Apply,
Limit (where it stops holding), Check (how to reject it) and typed edges to other cards (Requires, Supports,
Constrains) with the reason for each. [Connections](Connections.md) gives worked chains across groups.

Coverage is partial: cards were distilled from selected topics, not from complete volumes.

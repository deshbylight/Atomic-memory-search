## K01-18

**A player fantasy needs characteristic opportunities.** An intended role can be expressed through what players learn, feel and do; the setting alone does not demonstrate the promised experience.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: player fantasy; distinctive promise.
- Apply: Give an explorer, defender or negotiator a characteristic decision to make.
- Limit: A proposed role is an experience hypothesis, not an approved character or feature.
- Check: Remove the role name and identify which available action still expresses it.
- Requires [K01-01](Foundations.md#k01-01): A role promise needs a concrete experience goal.
- Requires [K06-20](EncounterComposition.md#k06-20): The corresponding approach needs usable opportunities in the actual space.

## K01-19

**Core and secondary mechanics imply different exposure.** Frequently used core activities and occasional secondary activities can need different amounts and kinds of level support.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: mechanics dynamics and experience; authoring cost.
- Apply: Mark routine opportunities separately from specialized shortcuts or optional uses.
- Limit: Frequency alone does not establish enjoyment or justify forcing every activity into every room.
- Check: Trace ordinary play and identify where each claimed core activity actually recurs.
- Supports [K01-18](GameplayScope.md#k01-18): Repeated opportunities can sustain the promised player role.
- Requires [K06-07](SpatialDesign.md#k06-07): Supported activities must fit their movement and interaction metrics.

## K01-20

**An integrated slice tests more than an isolated prototype.** A prototype can test user role, implementation or presentation separately; an integrated slice must also make those aspects work together through the production pipeline.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: scope and exclusions; authoring cost; performance and feasibility.
- Apply: Name the aspect each prototype proves and the integration still to test.
- Limit: A polished image or isolated technical success does not establish a feasible complete experience.
- Check: Follow one representative interaction through authoring, implementation and presentation; identify untested joins.
- Requires [K16-02](ResearchAndProduction.md#k16-02): Choose a focused prototype around the uncertain dependency.
- Requires [K06-01](SpatialDesign.md#k06-01): Spatial claims still need a playable test at player scale.

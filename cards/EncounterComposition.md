## K06-19

**A roster matrix exposes questions rather than mandatory slots.** Comparing speed, resilience, range and behavior can reveal duplicate roles or unexplored combinations in an enemy roster.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: enemy combinations.
- Apply: Ask what a missing role would add before creating it.
- Limit: Every empty matrix cell need not become a new enemy.
- Check: Compare two similar entries and identify the distinct player response each demands.
- Requires [K03-19](EncounterTactics.md#k03-19): Roster differences need meaningful tactical relationships, not only numerical differences.
- Constrains [K01-01](Foundations.md#k01-01): Roster expansion should serve the intended experience rather than completeness.

## K06-20

**A supported approach needs usable opportunities.** Claimed approaches need relevant objects, positions or targets in the actual arena.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: position and cover.
- Apply: Locate the objects, positions or targets each intended approach can use.
- Limit: An opportunity for a role does not establish that it can complete the encounter alone.
- Check: Try the least-supported intended approach with its actual resources and cooperation needs.
- Requires [K06-07](SpatialDesign.md#k06-07): Reach and movement bound those opportunities.
- Constrains [K09-21](InteractiveChoices.md#k09-21): An opportunity to contribute is distinct from an independently sufficient solution.

## K13-25

**Tooth contact surfaces reveal different processing actions.** Cutting tooth edges can shear food while broad grinding contacts process it differently; the opposing surfaces matter as well as isolated tooth shape.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: feeding; Mouth, teeth, claws and feeding.
- Apply: Draw upper and lower teeth meeting around a representative food item.
- Limit: A pointed tooth alone proves neither a complete diet nor a hunting behavior.
- Check: Close the mouth and inspect which surfaces cut, crush or grind the food.
- Requires [K13-26](CreatureFeeding.md#k13-26): Tooth contact depends on the movement available at the jaw.
- Supports [K07-18](EcologicalCommunities.md#k07-18): Food processing can help distinguish resource use among coexisting creatures.

## K13-26

**Jaw movement must suit the feeding operation.** A jaw joint permits and restricts movements: shearing, lateral grinding and fore-and-aft gnawing use different contact paths.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: Joint range, compression and extension; Mouth, teeth, claws and feeding.
- Apply: Animate one feeding stroke before exaggerating jaw shape or tusk placement.
- Limit: These are comparative patterns, not identical joint mechanics across all mammals.
- Check: Track the lower jaw through the full stroke and check tooth clearance.
- Requires [K13-01](CharactersAndMotion.md#k13-01): The feeding activity needs a body that can perform it.
- Supports [K13-09](CharactersAndMotion.md#k13-09): Intermediate positions expose collisions that endpoints can hide.

## K13-27

**A cellulose diet can depend on microbial partners.** Ruminants obtain usable nutrition from fibrous plant material through microbial fermentation in specialized digestive compartments, alongside their own digestion and absorption.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: feeding; Mouth, teeth, claws and feeding.
- Apply: Explain how a bulky plant diet is processed rather than only naming the food.
- Limit: Not every herbivore has the same stomach arrangement or fermentation site.
- Check: Remove the microbial processing step and identify which food remains usable.
- Requires [K07-02](PhysicalWorld.md#k07-02): A microbial partner is a dependency in the feeding relationship.
- Requires [K07-16](EcologicalCommunities.md#k07-16): The proposed diet still needs a sufficient food-energy input.

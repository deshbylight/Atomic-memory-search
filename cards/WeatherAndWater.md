## K07-20

**Water moderates temperature through thermal inertia.** A large water body changes temperature more slowly than land under comparable heating; maritime influence can damp seasonal temperature swings.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: seasons; Energy budgets and temperature.
- Apply: Compare coastal and inland seasonal conditions before assigning identical vegetation.
- Limit: Winds, currents and barriers can limit maritime influence; proximity alone is insufficient.
- Check: Trace the arriving air and compare seasonal temperature ranges.
- Constrains [K07-11](PhysicalWorld.md#k07-11): Seasonal extremes affect the resources a habitat can sustain.
- Supports [K07-02](PhysicalWorld.md#k07-02): Temperature conditions help explain local ecological dependencies.

## K07-21

**Evaporation exchanges water for cooling.** Evaporation consumes energy and cools the remaining water; drier moving air generally promotes evaporation, while condensation releases latent heat.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: water cycle; Energy budgets and temperature; Humidity, clouds, precipitation and fog.
- Apply: Give a cooling wet surface both water supply and air exposure.
- Limit: A damp appearance alone establishes neither sustained evaporation nor a temperature.
- Check: Reduce airflow or increase humidity; revisit water loss and cooling.
- Requires [K07-01](PhysicalWorld.md#k07-01): Sustained wetness needs an incoming water path or finite storage.
- Constrains [K07-20](WeatherAndWater.md#k07-20): Latent heat exchange differs from storing sensible heat.

## K07-22

**A rain shadow needs a moisture-bearing air path.** Air lifted across a mountain can cool, condense and lose moisture; descending leeward air can warm and leave a drier rain shadow.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: Atmospheric circulation and prevailing winds; Humidity, clouds, precipitation and fog; Regional rain shadows and microclimates.
- Apply: Draw moisture source, prevailing wind and ridge before placing wet and dry slopes.
- Limit: A ridge alone does not guarantee a desert or fixed rainfall ratio.
- Check: Reverse the arriving wind and reconsider which slope receives rain.
- Supports [K07-08](PhysicalWorld.md#k07-08): Rainfall supplies a possible cause of changing slope wetness.
- Supports [K07-02](PhysicalWorld.md#k07-02): Moisture differences can change dependencies across the ridge.

## K07-23

**A migrating rain belt can produce wet and dry seasons.** Seasonal movement of tropical convergence and associated wind belts can change when a region receives rising moist air and rain.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: seasons; Atmospheric circulation and prevailing winds; Humidity, clouds, precipitation and fog.
- Apply: Compare a settlement during the wet and dry parts of its year.
- Limit: Tropical seasonality need not follow four temperate seasons or uniform annual timing.
- Check: Move the rain belt and trace how water availability changes.
- Supports [K07-21](WeatherAndWater.md#k07-21): Humidity changes alter the conditions for evaporative water loss.
- Constrains [K07-11](PhysicalWorld.md#k07-11): A dry interval can change the resources sustaining inhabitants.

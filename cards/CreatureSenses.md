## K13-20

**A sense needs the right stimulus at its receptor.** Sensory receptors respond to particular stimulus types within receptive fields; reception and the later interpretation of a signal are separate processes.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: senses; Senses, eyes and orientation.
- Apply: Trace stimulus, transmission path and receptor before assigning a creature detection ability.
- Limit: A large sense organ alone establishes neither unlimited range nor correct identification.
- Check: Remove the stimulus or block its path; reconsider what information remains.
- Constrains [K03-07](ActionsAndCombat.md#k03-07): Encounter detection needs a plausible channel for its evidence.
- Supports [K02-12](PlayerExperience.md#k02-12): Receiving a signal does not guarantee attention to it.

## K13-21

**Touch sensitivity and spatial resolution differ.** Small tactile receptive fields and dense receptor coverage can support finer spatial discrimination; detecting contact does not imply locating two nearby contacts separately.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: senses; Senses, eyes and orientation.
- Apply: Distinguish a sensitive probing surface from a broadly sensitive body covering.
- Limit: Human fingertip resolution is not a measurement for every animal or body region.
- Check: Compare one contact with two nearby contacts before claiming precise localization.
- Requires [K13-20](CreatureSenses.md#k13-20): Spatial information still depends on the activated receptor fields.
- Supports [K13-01](CharactersAndMotion.md#k13-01): A probing activity needs suitable contact surfaces and posture.

## K13-22

**Dim-light sensitivity does not guarantee color discrimination.** In rod-and-cone vision, rods support sensitivity in dim light while cones support color discrimination under brighter conditions.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: senses; Senses, eyes and orientation.
- Apply: Check which markings a night-active creature can distinguish at its working light level.
- Limit: Photoreceptor arrangements differ among species; darkness supplies no light to detect.
- Check: Reduce illumination and test identification separately from detection of a shape.
- Requires [K13-20](CreatureSenses.md#k13-20): Visual reception depends on light reaching suitable receptors.
- Supports [K10-07](VisualLanguage.md#k10-07): Contour can carry distinctions when internal color markings fail.

## K13-23

**Olfaction needs transported molecules and matching receptors.** An odor is detected when released molecules reach responsive chemical receptors; organisms differ in which odorants their receptor systems can recognize.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: senses; Senses, eyes and orientation.
- Apply: Give a scent-tracking creature an emitting target and a delivery path.
- Limit: Human inability to smell something does not establish its absence for another species.
- Check: Seal the emitting surface or interrupt transport; revisit the claimed detection.
- Requires [K13-20](CreatureSenses.md#k13-20): Chemical detection still needs the relevant stimulus at its receptor.
- Supports [K07-02](PhysicalWorld.md#k07-02): Odor relationships can connect food, prey, predators and mates.

## K13-24

**Hearing depends on frequency as well as amplitude.** Hearing systems differ in frequency sensitivity; a sound audible to one species can lie outside the hearing range of another.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: senses; Senses, eyes and orientation.
- Apply: Specify the listener and call frequency before deciding who can hear a signal.
- Limit: Loudness alone does not establish audibility, localization or understanding.
- Check: Keep amplitude fixed and vary frequency; compare the proposed listeners.
- Requires [K13-20](CreatureSenses.md#k13-20): The pressure wave must reach a responsive sensory system.
- Supports [K03-07](ActionsAndCombat.md#k03-07): An encounter detection rule can communicate its audible evidence.

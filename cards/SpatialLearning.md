## K06-15

**A changed challenge tests retrieval beyond repetition.** Teaching, prompted repetition and a changed challenge with fewer prompts test different stages of using an activity.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: teaching testing and variation; encounter sequences.
- Apply: Keep the learned action but change its context before claiming transferable understanding.
- Limit: Three beats are a planning pattern, not a universal learning schedule.
- Check: Compare prompted success with an unfamiliar application; record which cue the player needed.
- Requires [K02-13](PlayerExperience.md#k02-13): Demonstration alone does not establish independent execution.
- Requires [K02-11](PlayerExperience.md#k02-11): Changed cues can compete with previously learned associations.

## K06-16

**Nonlinear pacing depends on possible orders.** When players choose routes, layout and travel costs shape pacing without guaranteeing one authored event sequence.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: beats and contrast; encounter sequences; repeat traversal.
- Apply: Trace several permitted orders, including a return, before predicting an experience curve.
- Limit: One intended traversal cannot represent all paths or participant timings.
- Check: Try a reversed approach and a separated teammate; identify missing prerequisites or misplaced recovery.
- Requires [K06-10](SpatialDesign.md#k06-10): Allowed orders must preserve the activity prerequisites.
- Requires [K06-04](SpatialDesign.md#k06-04): A return can change access and the next activity sequence.

## K06-17

**A noticed threat can attract or repel.** The same visible threat may invite engagement or encourage avoidance; noticing a cue does not determine the chosen route.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: orientation cues; entry and survey.
- Apply: Separate cue detection, interpreted meaning and route choice when evaluating an encounter entrance.
- Limit: Designer estimates of guidance strength are not measured success probabilities.
- Check: Observe the chosen exit, then ask what the threat suggested before explaining its intended role.
- Requires [K02-12](PlayerExperience.md#k02-12): First establish whether the cue received attention.
- Supports [K16-17](PlaytestPractice.md#k16-17): Comprehension questions can separate noticing from interpretation.

## K06-18

**A destination label is not a direction.** Identification marks where a visitor has arrived; directional information indicates which way to travel at a route choice.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: landmarks; orientation cues.
- Apply: At each junction, distinguish naming the current place from naming the path to another.
- Limit: A sign can be treated as decoration or misunderstood even when visible.
- Check: Ask a newcomer to locate the named destination from the junction, without an explanatory prompt.
- Requires [K06-14](SpatialDesign.md#k06-14): A destination needs a relationship to the current location.
- Requires [K06-17](SpatialLearning.md#k06-17): Noticed information still needs its meaning checked.

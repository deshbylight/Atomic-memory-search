# Cross-domain concept chains

[Knowledge index](README.md)

Each arrow below has a condition. These are ways to reason about a proposal, not
fixed world laws. The card edges are directed; reverse
lookup means finding incoming edges, not assuming that an implication is reversible.

## Wet underground occupation

[K07-01](PhysicalWorld.md#k07-01) water paths ->
[K11-03](BuiltWorld.md#k11-03) support and maintained access ->
[K12-02](EnvironmentArt.md#k12-02) localized surface variation ->
[K12-04](EnvironmentArt.md#k12-04) readable integrated scene.

Condition: water enters the proposed structure. Explain its storage and exit before
placing occupation or dressing. A damp cue should support that explanation; it must
not hide the exit or imply a mechanic that has not been approved.
Rejecting probe: trace the water path in section and identify the route in the player view.

## Cooperative exploration

[K05-03](CooperativePlay.md#k05-03) different discoveries ->
[K02-01](PlayerExperience.md#k02-01) distinctive recall cues ->
[K06-02](SpatialDesign.md#k06-02) recognizable route decisions ->
[K15-04](InteractionAndAccess.md#k15-04) legible shared state.

Condition: participants separate or arrive at different times. A landmark supports
recall, topology provides a route, and shared information explains the meeting point.
Rejecting probe: let separated players agree on a destination with voice disabled.

Another chain: [K01-12](Foundations.md#k01-12) contingent plan -> [K01-13](Foundations.md#k01-13) distinguishable situations -> [K05-03](CooperativePlay.md#k05-03) different discoveries.
Condition: decisions depend on hidden prior events. Rejecting probe: change an unseen
event and check that the plan does not use information unavailable to its player.

## Inhabited ruin

[K08-03](Societies.md#k08-03) making, use and adaptation ->
[K11-04](BuiltWorld.md#k11-04) phases of occupation ->
[K12-03](EnvironmentArt.md#k12-03) localized prop evidence ->
[K09-04](Narrative.md#k09-04) recoverable event sequence.

Condition: later occupants reuse an earlier structure. Separate original purpose,
damage and repair so props communicate activity instead of generic age.
Rejecting probe: ask a player to reconstruct the sequence from the actual traces.

## Readable creature encounter

[K07-02](PhysicalWorld.md#k07-02) habitat dependencies ->
[K13-01](CharactersAndMotion.md#k13-01) feasible body activity ->
[K13-04](CharactersAndMotion.md#k13-04) visible commitment ->
[K14-04](Audiovisual.md#k14-04) coordinated cues ->
[K15-03](InteractionAndAccess.md#k15-03) redundant essential meaning.

Condition: the creature's activity becomes a player encounter. Body, motion and effects
should describe the same action; losing colour or sound should not remove its only cue.
Rejecting probe: identify the action before its outcome in a crowded encounter.

## Return play and recovery

[K01-04](Foundations.md#k01-04) retained world changes ->
[K06-04](SpatialDesign.md#k06-04) state-aware return tests ->
[K03-04](ActionsAndCombat.md#k03-04) cost of recovery ->
[K04-04](EconomyAndProgression.md#k04-04) repeated cost observation.

Condition: a location or recovery activity is revisited. Use retained state and the
approved death/loot contract; evaluate whether later visits still contain decisions.
Rejecting probe: trace repeated recovery without a hidden reset or timed loot removal.

## Selective decay and surviving evidence

[K07-07](PhysicalWorld.md#k07-07) mineral-dependent alteration ->
[K12-06](EnvironmentArt.md#k12-06) selective surface loss ->
[K11-04](BuiltWorld.md#k11-04) distinguishable ruin phases.

Condition: water exposure and material composition explain the selective loss. The
surviving pattern can support a history hypothesis; it does not uniquely prove one.
Rejecting probe: account for the material that survives beside the missing material,
then check whether later repair or reuse contradicts the inferred event order.

## Contribution incentives and perceived cost

[K05-05](CooperativePlay.md#k05-05) private cost and shared benefit ->
[K04-05](EconomyAndProgression.md#k04-05) forgone alternative ->
[K02-02](PlayerExperience.md#k02-02) perceived stakes.

Condition: a participant gives up an individually useful option to produce a shared
benefit. Compare that alternative before interpreting noncontribution as selfishness.
Rejecting probe: compare contribution and withholding under identical partner actions;
observe actual choices rather than treating the payoff model as a behavioral prediction.

## Motifs across constructed cloth

[K10-08](VisualLanguage.md#k10-08) motif and containing surface ->
[K13-07](CharactersAndMotion.md#k13-07) garment fullness and seams ->
[K12-10](EnvironmentArt.md#k12-10) weave versus applied pattern.

Condition: a visual family extends onto clothing or furnishings. Relate ornament to
surface boundaries while keeping cloth construction, relief and color distinguishable. Spacing branch: [K10-15](VisualLanguage.md#k10-15) background intervals -> [K02-15](PlayerExperience.md#k02-15) perceptual grouping -> [K10-01](VisualLanguage.md#k10-01) mass hierarchy. Compare changed gaps before treating repeated detail as one mass.
Rejecting probe: trace a fold to its support and inspect the motif at a seam; then
reduce viewing scale and check whether the large form survives the loss of fine weave.

## River channels and flood aftermath

[K07-13](PhysicalWorld.md#k07-13) channel pattern ->
[K07-12](PhysicalWorld.md#k07-12) selective transport ->
[K12-11](EnvironmentArt.md#k12-11) sorted flood deposits ->
[K12-02](EnvironmentArt.md#k12-02) localized surface variation.

Condition: water carries sediment out of a channel. Relate the deposits to changing
flow and particle properties; a uniform mud layer cannot explain every surface.
Rejecting probe: trace overflow from the channel and justify the texture changes,
including any later disturbance that disrupts the expected pattern.

## Damage order and conflicting testimony

[K07-14](PhysicalWorld.md#k07-14) relative event order ->
[K12-12](EnvironmentArt.md#k12-12) repair and damage intersections ->
[K09-10](Narrative.md#k09-10) questionable account ->
[K03-08](ActionsAndCombat.md#k03-08) inspectable evidence.

Condition: an account describes events that left overlapping physical traces.
Separate what those contacts establish from dates or motives that remain uncertain.
Rejecting probe: compare competing accounts against the same contacts; identify
which account contradicts observed order and which differences remain unresolved.

## Arrival, turning and body motion

[K03-11](ActionsAndCombat.md#k03-11) slowing near a target ->
[K13-11](CharactersAndMotion.md#k13-11) steering response ->
[K13-01](CharactersAndMotion.md#k13-01) feasible support and contact.

Condition: a movement proposal needs both approach and directional changes.
Distinguish the desired trajectory from the body's ability to execute it.
Rejecting probe: compare approaches and reversals at equal top speed, then inspect
whether the selected trajectory can preserve the proposed body's support contacts.

## Gift obligations and anticipated consequences

[K08-12](Societies.md#k08-12) accepted reciprocal obligation ->
[K09-11](Narrative.md#k09-11) anticipated unresolved consequence ->
[K02-01](PlayerExperience.md#k02-01) retrievable earlier cue.

Condition: a gift establishes an understood return and interval. That obligation
can support suspense only if the audience knows what remains unresolved.
Rejecting probe: list the clues available before the expected return; distinguish
an anticipated consequence from a breach that arrives without preparation.

## Audio disclosure and reachable evidence

[K14-14](Audiovisual.md#k14-14) sound across a picture transition ->
[K09-12](Narrative.md#k09-12) disclosure order versus event chronology ->
[K06-10](SpatialDesign.md#k06-10) prerequisites mapped into traversable space.

Condition: sound reveals information before or after the associated image during
a sequence connected to exploration. Keep the reveal's timing and access explicit.
Rejecting probe: compare sound and picture timelines with permitted discovery
routes; identify any required inference that depends on evidence a route can miss.

## Audience coverage and access claims

[K16-13](ResearchAndProduction.md#k16-13) planned subgroup comparisons ->
[K16-12](ResearchAndProduction.md#k16-12) recruitment and nonresponse limits ->
[K15-03](InteractionAndAccess.md#k15-03) essential meaning across visual differences.

Condition: an evaluation makes claims about access across an intended audience.
Plan relevant groups while checking who the recruitment channel excludes.
Rejecting probe: show which observations support each access claim; more volunteers
from the same restricted audience cannot establish coverage of a missing group.

## Alternate approaches and shared coordination

[K01-10](Foundations.md#k01-10) local connections versus overall travel ->
[K06-13](SpatialDesign.md#k06-13) connections between prominent routes ->
[K05-02](CooperativePlay.md#k05-02) communication bottlenecks.

Condition: teammates can change approach when a main route is blocked. Compare
the actual detour with the information needed to choose and coordinate it.
Rejecting probe: block one approach and trace the alternatives; a shorter route
does not establish that separated teammates can recognize and use it together.

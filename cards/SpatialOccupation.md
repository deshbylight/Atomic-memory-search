## K06-21

**Current occupation can reinterpret an inherited plan.** The builders of a place and its current inhabitants can have different needs; present activity can reuse or obstruct an inherited layout.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: original function; current use; history in layout.
- Apply: Overlay current access, storage and work on the earlier plan.
- Limit: A changed use does not require every original feature to disappear.
- Check: Remove the current occupation layer and identify what the older layout still explains.
- Requires [K11-04](BuiltWorld.md#k11-04): Distinguish construction, abandonment and reuse phases.
- Supports [K06-03](SpatialDesign.md#k06-03): Occupation can explain obstructions that change encounter movement.

## K06-22

**Player intervention needs a visible inhabitant response.** A place can connect its past and present with what players may change next, including how current inhabitants react.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: current use; environmental storytelling; meaningful change on return.
- Apply: Pair one possible intervention with an observable local response.
- Limit: This planning question does not prescribe hostility, a scripted sequence or automatic restoration.
- Check: Compare the location before and after intervention; identify the affected activity and response.
- Requires [K01-03](Foundations.md#k01-03): The response needs an inhabitant aim and perceived change.
- Requires [K09-24](PuzzleRecovery.md#k09-24): Later interactions must agree with the changed situation.

## K06-23

**An unclaimed reward needs a reason it remains.** A valuable object in a visited place raises questions about who wants it, who can reach it and why it has not already been taken.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: inhabitant logistics; environmental storytelling.
- Apply: Relate one reward to a specific deterrent, owner or access condition.
- Limit: Not every reward needs a combat guard or a locked door.
- Check: Trace an inhabitant with a reason to want the object; explain why it remains.
- Requires [K08-03](Societies.md#k08-03): Material value and use need a social context.
- Requires [K06-21](SpatialOccupation.md#k06-21): Current occupation determines who plausibly encounters the object.

## K06-24

**Route complications can reflect nearby activity.** Travel complications can be related to neighboring locations and factions rather than chosen independently of the route.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: current use; inhabitant logistics.
- Apply: Connect an obstruction or encounter to an adjacent activity and motive.
- Limit: A thematic connection does not make every journey event mandatory or predictable.
- Check: Move the activity elsewhere and identify which route complication loses its cause.
- Requires [K06-21](SpatialOccupation.md#k06-21): Current inhabitants and uses establish the relevant activity.
- Supports [K01-03](Foundations.md#k01-03): Intersecting local aims can produce a travel situation without a unique script.

## K11-15

**An entrance route can expose private circulation.** An entrance opening directly into the main stair can expose private circulation; intermediate rooms or screens can separate access.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: public and private boundaries; Public, communal, private and restricted zones; Entrances, thresholds and transitional spaces.
- Apply: Trace a visitor route independently from residents reaching private rooms.
- Limit: Privacy is a chosen requirement, not a universal rule of house planning.
- Check: From the entrance, mark every directly reachable stair and private threshold.
- Requires [K06-12](SpatialDesign.md#k06-12): Route hierarchy depends on intended users and access.
- Supports [K11-01](BuiltWorld.md#k11-01): A privacy requirement gives the entrance a functional programme.

## K11-16

**An entrance is also a maintenance transition.** A vestibule receives dirt and weather exposure, making cleanability and damage resistance relevant to its finishes and furnishings.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: Storage, workshops, sanitation and maintenance; climate response; wear.
- Apply: Differentiate exposed entrance surfaces from protected rooms by their maintenance demands.
- Limit: Historical material preferences are not universal prescriptions.
- Check: Trace dirt, water and cleaning access; reject a delicate finish with no maintenance explanation.
- Supports [K10-24](CompositionStudies.md#k10-24): Expected contact can explain a protected lower wall.
- Supports [K11-15](InteriorSequence.md#k11-15): A transition can serve maintenance and access separation together.

## K11-17

**Removing supports changes more than the floor plan.** A reused interior can replace columns with larger spanning arches while retaining traces of earlier support positions.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: adaptation; interpretation from surviving traces; Reuse, rebuilding and historical layers.
- Apply: Draw old and new support systems separately before designing an opened-up ruin.
- Limit: A surviving trace does not establish the stability of the replacement.
- Check: Trace the new bearing path and identify evidence distinguishing replacement from simple collapse.
- Requires [K11-11](BuiltWorld.md#k11-11): The altered supports still need a credible bearing below.
- Supports [K12-12](EnvironmentArt.md#k12-12): Physical intersections can constrain the order of alterations.

## K11-18

**Linked buildings can retain different construction phases.** Neighboring buildings can become a compound through openings in shared walls and added connecting galleries while retaining distinct internal plans.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: adaptation; Reuse, rebuilding and historical layers; Readable original function and changed use.
- Apply: Assign each connection a construction phase and compare circulation before and after linking.
- Limit: Irregularity alone cannot date an addition or identify its purpose.
- Check: Remove the proposed later links on a diagram; check whether the earlier plans remain intelligible.
- Supports [K11-04](BuiltWorld.md#k11-04): Separate phases can explain relationships in a reused compound.
- Supports [K06-06](SpatialDesign.md#k06-06): A later connection can change the cost of returning.

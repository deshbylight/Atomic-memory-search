## K06-25

**A place to rest needs usable recovery conditions.** Rest can require time, light and freedom from approaching danger.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: escape and recovery; safety expectations.
- Apply: Name the conditions and a reachable shelter.
- Limit: This approves neither dungeon rest nor hub invulnerability.
- Check: Trace arrival and uninterrupted recovery under actual threats.
- Requires [K03-04](ActionsAndCombat.md#k03-04): Recovery has costs and exposure.
- Requires [K06-05](SpatialDesign.md#k06-05): Visible shelter must also be reachable.

## K06-26

**Service adjacency can share support and create conflicts.** Neighboring activities can share heat or handling, while incompatible uses or fire exposure can justify separation.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: inhabitant logistics; service adjacency.
- Apply: Name the useful transfer and the cost of adjacency.
- Limit: A historical functional example supplies neither engineering guidance nor approved game hazards or services.
- Check: Trace one beneficial transfer and one incompatible interaction.
- Requires [K11-01](BuiltWorld.md#k11-01): Activities and circulation establish what needs connecting.
- Constrains [K04-04](EconomyAndProgression.md#k04-04): Service placement changes repeated handling and travel costs.

## K06-27

**Routes and activity changes can distinguish districts.** Routes can define wards, and changes of use can distinguish related zones without requiring enclosing walls.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: maps and spatial memory; districts and thresholds.
- Apply: Mark the activity, access or circulation change across a boundary.
- Limit: A city-scale example does not require separate districts or green space in a small hub.
- Check: Cross both ways and distinguish the uses and connections, beyond their labels.
- Supports [K06-14](SpatialDesign.md#k06-14): Distinct connected areas can contribute to a mental map.
- Requires [K06-12](SpatialDesign.md#k06-12): A boundary must preserve the intended circulation hierarchy.

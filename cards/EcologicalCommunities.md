## K07-16

**Trophic transfers limit sustained consumer populations.** Energy leaves usable food pathways at successive trophic transfers, limiting how many feeding levels a given input can sustain.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: food webs; Energy sources and primary production; Food webs and trophic limits.
- Apply: Trace a predator population back through prey to its primary energy input.
- Limit: No fixed transfer percentage or biomass ordering applies to every ecosystem.
- Check: Remove one energy input; identify which consumer populations lose support.
- Requires [K07-09](PhysicalWorld.md#k07-09): Nutrient recycling does not replace energy input.
- Supports [K07-11](PhysicalWorld.md#k07-11): Resource support constrains the population a habitat can maintain.

## K07-17

**A detrital food web still needs an organic input.** Decomposers and detritivores can support food webs based on dead organic matter; dark habitats can also receive imported organic matter or chemical energy.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: underground energy sources; food webs; Nutrient cycles and decomposition; Underground ecosystems and energy budgets.
- Apply: For a dark habitat, distinguish local production from delivered remains and detritus.
- Limit: Darkness alone supplies no energy, and a food-web arrow is not a measured flux.
- Check: Stop the incoming material; explain what production or stored food remains.
- Requires [K07-16](EcologicalCommunities.md#k07-16): Detrital consumers also face losses during energy transfer.
- Supports [K07-15](PhysicalWorld.md#k07-15): A feeding network can extend beyond visible fungal structures.

## K07-18

**A niche includes resource use and species interactions.** Species sharing a habitat can differ in the resources they use and their interactions; complete overlap in limiting resource use can prevent stable coexistence.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: habitat; symbiosis and competition; Competition, predation, symbiosis and disease.
- Apply: Give coexisting creatures different feeding times, food sources or usable microhabitats.
- Limit: A different appearance does not establish a different niche; changing conditions can alter competition.
- Check: Remove the proposed partition and check which limiting resource both populations now require.
- Requires [K07-02](PhysicalWorld.md#k07-02): Resource use includes relationships with other organisms.
- Constrains [K07-11](PhysicalWorld.md#k07-11): Coexistence still depends on the resources available locally.

## K07-19

**Recovery differs when soil and survivors remain.** Colonization of bare substrate differs from recovery where soil and remnants survive; these starting conditions influence the succession pathway.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: Disturbance, succession and recovery; succession; recovery.
- Apply: Distinguish a stripped rock surface from an abandoned plot retaining soil and roots.
- Limit: Succession is not a universal timetable or a guarantee of returning to the earlier community.
- Check: Compare substrates and surviving sources of recolonization before assigning equal regrowth.
- Requires [K07-03](PhysicalWorld.md#k07-03): Growth still depends on water, climate and changing shade.
- Supports [K07-18](EcologicalCommunities.md#k07-18): Changing resources can replace one set of occupants with another.

## K01-14

**An established solution can remove the need for chance.** When available knowledge, resources and circumstances already settle an action, adding a random check can disregard the solution the player supplied.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: constraints and opportunity; skill and chance.
- Apply: Separate unresolved risk from an approach that already addresses the obstacle.
- Limit: Tabletop resolution advice does not prescribe dice or automatic success for an action game.
- Check: Explain what remains uncertain after the proposed preparation and method.
- Requires [K01-02](Foundations.md#k01-02): The method must satisfy the actual action prerequisites.
- Supports [K09-21](InteractiveChoices.md#k09-21): A sufficient alternative can resolve a goal through its own conditions.

## K01-15

**Resolution can separate completion from exposure and cost.** An action result can distinguish whether a goal was achieved from what danger, disclosure or resource cost followed.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: win loss and continuation; cross-system consequences.
- Apply: Describe completion, exposure and cost separately for each relevant result.
- Limit: Partial outcomes need defined rules; they do not guarantee progress after every failure.
- Check: Compare two results that complete the same task but leave different consequences.
- Requires [K01-02](Foundations.md#k01-02): Distinct consequences need distinct resulting states.
- Supports [K09-23](PuzzleRecovery.md#k09-23): A response can identify the consequence rather than report only failure.

## K01-16

**Chance events and participant choices are different branches.** A chance event assigns probabilities to outcomes; a participant chooses an action. Even fixed participant strategies can therefore lead to different final results.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: game states; skill and chance; predictability and surprise.
- Apply: Mark who chooses each branch and which branches use a probability model.
- Limit: An unknown opponent choice is not automatically a random event with known odds.
- Check: Hold participant plans fixed, then vary only the chance outcome.
- Requires [K01-13](Foundations.md#k01-13): Hidden information must remain distinct from a modeled chance event.
- Supports [K04-03](EconomyAndProgression.md#k04-03): Repeated strategy comparisons must account for chance-driven outcome variation.

## K01-17

**One risk preference does not rank every gamble.** Preferring a certain payment to a gamble of equal expected value does not, by itself, rank every pair of risky offers.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: meaningful alternatives; skill and chance.
- Apply: Compare the specific offers and consequences rather than infer all choices from one risk label.
- Limit: Money-lottery definitions do not establish preferences about health, discovery or cooperation.
- Check: Show which additional preference evidence distinguishes two proposed risky alternatives.
- Requires [K04-09](EconomyAndProgression.md#k04-09): The compared offers need their actual outcome distributions.
- Constrains [K04-03](EconomyAndProgression.md#k04-03): A risk label alone does not establish which option players will favor.

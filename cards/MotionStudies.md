## K13-16

**A quadruped leap begins before takeoff.** In a quadruped leap, hind-limb flexion prepares a rapid extension that lifts and projects the body; the amount of folding differs among animals.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: Locomotion, balance and support; anticipation; Anticipation, action and recovery.
- Apply: Block preparation, push-off and clearance before the airborne silhouette.
- Limit: One crouch depth or duration does not describe every animal or jump.
- Check: Remove the preparation and inspect how takeoff could generate the shown trajectory.
- Requires [K13-01](CharactersAndMotion.md#k13-01): The body and contacts must support the proposed propulsion.
- Supports [K13-04](CharactersAndMotion.md#k13-04): Preparation can expose an approaching action to another participant.

## K13-17

**Galloping contacts differ from a diagonal trot.** A horse canter has three footfall beats and suspension; a faster gallop can separate the diagonal contacts into four beats, with a leading side.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: Gait, stride and foot contact; Cycles, variation and transitions.
- Apply: Write a contact timeline before speeding up a horse cycle.
- Limit: Other species and transitions need their own gait observations.
- Check: Mark each footfall and suspension; reject an unchanged trot sped up.
- Constrains [K13-15](CharactersAndMotion.md#k13-15): The diagonal trot is a comparison, not every fast gait.
- Supports [K14-04](Audiovisual.md#k14-04): Hoof sounds should agree with the visible footfall pattern.

## K13-18

**A traveling body wave needs successive phases.** An undulating elongated body can carry bends along its length; turning every segment together loses the traveling wave.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: Locomotion, balance and support; Cycles, variation and transitions.
- Apply: Track one bend through successive poses rather than moving a rigid curve.
- Limit: A traveling shape alone does not establish traction or propulsion on every surface.
- Check: Mark adjacent body regions and compare when each reaches its bend maximum.
- Requires [K13-01](CharactersAndMotion.md#k13-01): Locomotion still needs an explained interaction with its surroundings.
- Requires [K13-09](CharactersAndMotion.md#k13-09): Phase progression depends on intervals as well as selected poses.

## K13-19

**A moving straight rope needs visible displacement evidence.** A straight uniform rope moving along its own length can appear stationary; a traveling strand, kink or contrasting detail can reveal its motion.

- Kind: general guidance. Focus: staging.
- Apply: Test the rope at the intended viewing distance with and without a trackable detail.
- Limit: An added wave must remain compatible with the rope tension being represented.
- Check: Hide the hands and check whether an observer can identify motion direction.
- Constrains [K10-07](VisualLanguage.md#k10-07): An unchanged silhouette may omit longitudinal displacement.
- Requires [K13-09](CharactersAndMotion.md#k13-09): A tracked detail needs consistent progression between positions.

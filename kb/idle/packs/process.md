# process

## game balancing and tuning
- [procedure] 1) Model cost and income curves in a spreadsheet. 2) At each progression stage, compute time until the next meaningful purchase. 3) Check whether that interval stays roughly constant. 4) Flag stages where it spikes, since players are likely to quit there. When: when tuning an idle game's progression before a balance pass. Why: Measuring the interval targets what players actually feel rather than raw magnitude.
- [fact] Balancing progression is hard; spreadsheet models help, but iteration is required, and many tools are available.

## crafting and production queues
- [tradeoff] Artisan crafts consume ingredients at craft start and output at timer end; a queued quantity repeats while ingredients remain, so a mid-craft stop leaves input cost paid. When: Designing crafting with inputs and queued repeats. Why: Upfront consumption means interruptions waste inputs.

## playtest and testing
- [procedure] 1) Play as a new player. 2) Check whether a meaningful decision point is reached within 5 minutes. 3) Revise the opening if not. When: Auditing an early-game onboarding sequence. Why: New players decide quickly whether to keep playing.
